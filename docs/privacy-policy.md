# 🔒 ICIT GLOBAL PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Owner:** Iweo Global Inc. (Operating ICIT)  

---

Iweo Global Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Integrous Cohesion for Intelligent Technologies (ICIT) verification platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, share, and protect your personal information in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other global data security frameworks.

---

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

To provide objective technical validation registry services, we collect the following categories of information:

* **Account Information:** Name, email address, corporate affiliations, and development environment metadata (e.g., connected GitHub handles).

* **Identity Verification Data:** To prevent proxy testing, we utilize a third-party identity verification processor (Persona). Persona collects your government-issued identity documents and performs structural facial biometric analysis to match your identity. We do not store raw biometric profiles on our servers; we receive verification status tokens from Persona.

* **Technical Evaluation Data:** Code inputs, logic models, script submissions, execution times, and technical system telemetry submitted within the evaluation sandbox.

* **Transaction Information:** Billing details, regional tax identification numbers, and payment history processed securely by our Merchant of Record, Paddle. 

---

## 2. HOW WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION

We process data under the following legal bases:

* **Performance of a Contract:** To manage your account, execute sandbox tests, and record validation registry metrics.

* **Legal Obligation:** To verify tax compliance, prevent fraud, and cross-reference geographic purchasing eligibility.

* **Legitimate Interests:** To improve automated testing algorithms, track system uptime, and prevent platform abuse.

---

## 3. Data Escrow, Anonymization & Third-Party Processors

We never sell your personal data. To protect our technical assets and enforce structural transaction parameters—including our symmetrical monthly usage velocity walls and the contextually isolated Talent Vector Triad protocol sessions—individual candidate identities are protected by a secure cloud data escrow architecture:

* **The Anonymized Browse Layer:** Corporate subscribers paying the upfront access license parse strictly anonymized metrics, authenticated credentials, and capability indices. Personal metadata footprints remain fully encrypted and hidden.
* **The Decryption & Activation Event:** Personal registry info, contact records, and direct identity profiles are decrypted and unlocked for the corporate subscriber exclusively upon mutual agreement and successful confirmation of the Asset Activation Fee transaction via our payment rails.
* **Identity Verification:** Persona Inc. (for secure global biometric identity checks).
* **Payment Processing:** Paddle Inc. (acting as our compliant global Merchant of Record).
* **Hosting & Telemetry:** Enterprise secure cloud service providers (Modal Labs). 

---

## 4. YOUR GLOBAL PRIVACY RIGHTS

Regardless of geographic origin, users are granted robust control over their personal data. Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., UK/EU GDPR, California CCPA), you hold the following rights:

* **Right to Access:** The right to request an exported copy of your account profile and technical submission history.
* **Right to Rectification:** The right to correct inaccurate account fields.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** The right to request permanent deletion of your account and verification history, subject to statutory tax record-retention requirements held by our payment provider.
* **Right to Restrict Processing:** The right to halt or limit technical evaluation evaluations or automated identity matches.

---

## 5. DATA RETENTION

Account information and registry results remain active for the duration of your evaluation's 12-month validity window. 

Expired metrics are archived or anonymized within 90 days of expiration unless active account maintenance is requested. Secure identity validation logs are maintained only as long as necessary to prove verification validity.

---

## 6. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS AND SECURITY

Your data is securely processed in global data facilities using enterprise-grade encryption standard protocols (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). 

For users in the European Union or United Kingdom, data transfers outside the EEA are governed by approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
